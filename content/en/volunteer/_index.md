{{< intro >}}
Our annual competitions are all made possible by dedicated volunteers
{{< /intro >}}

If you or your organization are interested in volunteering for the league or in the cyber defense competition, please contact us at contact@neccdl.org.
If there is a specific team your interested in volunteering on check out their subpage for requirements, expectations and additional contact methods.

## White Team
Representatives who serve as competition officials, moderators, and rule enforcement in the various competition rooms.

- Supply and score Blue Team tasks in the form of competition injects
- Adjudicate the scoring for the competition

### Chief Judge
Responsible for final decisions concerning scoring.
The chief judge has no active or historical, professional or personal affiliation with any school in the league.

### Moderators
- Undertake moderator training offered by NECCDC
- Gain familiarity with using the required communication tools (e.g., Competition Discord)
- Submit questions and requests from the blue team members to the designated communication channels
- Check rosters to authenticate competitors & submitted equipment at check-in
- Ensure that competition rules are followed and any violations or situations of concern are reported to the White Team senior staff
- Submit survey feedback based on competition/team observations

### What Does volunteering with White Team Look Like?

White Team volunteers can either help write and grade injects or be in-room moderators! Writing injects can happen anytime before either qualifiers or regionals, but volunteers grading injects must be available during competition time to be involved with grading (remote or in-person). Moderators are present during competition and must be on-site.

## [Black Team]({{< ref "black-team" >}})
Black Team members are our competition technicians. They develop, deploy, and maintain a unique competition environment every year. They also configure remote access, Discord, the service scoring.

### Gray Team
Helps write technical injects alongside the White Team.

## Red Team
NECCDC Red Team act as simulated real-world attackers for our teams! NECCDC's Red Team has decades of cumulative experience in offensive security.

## Orange Team
These competition staff build an integrated scenario storyline and simulates user activities in a manner that integrates with and contextualizes Black and White team activities.
The team may also include individuals who act as employees, clients, or other external parties (e.g., C-suite personnel, law enforcement agents, etc.).
These actors may interact with infrastructure systems and the Blue Team members during the competition experience.

## Gold Team
The competition staff includes the Director, logistics, and sponsor relations coordinators.
