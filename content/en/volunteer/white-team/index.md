### What Does volunteering with White Team Look Like?

White Team volunteers can either help write and grade injects or be in-room moderators!
Writing injects can happen anytime before either qualifiers or regionals, but volunteers grading injects must be available during competition time to be involved with grading (remote or in-person).
Moderators are present during competition and must be on-site.

[2027 Season White Team Interest Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/1RrMdtdNx9yQa8AAIHtHJTAvyLSy0WkGMkMKaLn-btO4/edit?usp=drivesdk)

### Chief Judge

Responsible for final decisions concerning scoring.
The chief judge has no active or historical, professional or personal affiliation with any school in the league.

### Moderators

- Undertake moderator training offered by NECCDC
- Gain familiarity with using the required communication tools (e.g., Competition Discord)
- Submit questions and requests from the blue team members to the designated communication channels
- Check rosters to authenticate competitors & submitted equipment at check-in
- Ensure that competition rules are followed and any violations or situations of concern are reported to the White Team senior staff
- Submit survey feedback based on competition/team observations
