---
title: "Join the League"
description: Northeast institutions can join the NECCDL by having a coach register the team and pay the annual membership fee.
---

We welcome all interested institutions geographically located in the Northeast region to participate in the yearly NECCDC competition and the NECCDL league.

In order to compete in NECCDC, a team will need a staff or faculty member from its institution to represent the team, often referred to as a "coach".
A coach serves as the liaison between the team and competition officials and will need to complete the national registration, and pay the annual League membership fee.

Annual registration is $250; for institutions competing for the first time, it is only $100.

Travel costs, team gaps, or uncertainty, we understand. The Exhibition Team option allows you to participate only in the Qualifier competition for $75. It’s a low-cost way to gain experience, test your skills, and prepare for future seasons. More information can be [found here](/docs/neccdc/neccdc-exhibition-team).
