# What is NECCDC?
NECCDC is the Northeastern region of the National CCDC competition. NECCDC consists of collegiate teams (Blue Teams) from across the Northeast who compete for points. Points are gained by keeping services up, completing business objectives (injects), and defending against and reporting Red Team activity.

# What are injects?
Injects are business objectives teams must complete during the course of the competition. Injects can range from simple tasks such as creating a user for a new intern, to major network and infrastructure reconfigurations.

## How do I write a good inject?
The biggest thing is to make sure you're READING THE INJECT. Make note of exactly the deliverables it is asking for, and make sure to provide evidence of the work you completed. Ex. If an inject asks you to set up a new GPO *and* test it, make sure to provide screenshots of the GPO itself *AND* it succesfully working.
