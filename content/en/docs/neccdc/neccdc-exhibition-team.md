{{< intro >}}
We recognize that it can be difficult to get started competing in cybersecurity competitions.
{{< /intro >}}

Travel costs, incomplete teams, or knowledge uncertainty can make full participation in the NECCDC difficult.
The Exhibition Team option provides a low-commitment way to participate only in the Qualifier round, offering valuable experience without the full ccdc and league member obligations.

An exhibition team is recommended for the following:
- Institutions new to NECCDC
- Two-year schools or community colleges
- Cannot field a full team roster


### Requirements and Limitations
- $75 registration fee
- Participation only in the Beta test and Qualifier competition
  - The team is **not eligible** to advance to Regional or Nationals
- All team members must be active college students
  - Must be a Northeast institution
  - Any combination of undergraduate and postgraduate students permitted
  - Maximum of eight students per team
- No team coach required, only a primary point of contact
- Only one exhibition team allowed per institution
- Institutions that only have an exhibition team are not elegable to be a league member
- Students cannot be swapped or have overlap between the exhibition and a standard team


### Toned Down Rules
- No need for in-room moderator during qualifiers
