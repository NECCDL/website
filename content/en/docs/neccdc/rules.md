NECCDC subscribes to the National CCDC Rules, which all teams must follow.

https://www.nationalccdc.org/rules.html [^first]


### National Rules Amendments

#### 2. Team Composition
- Team rosters must be submitted before published deadlines, and all team members must meet all stated eligibility requirements.
- Any changes to the team rosters can be made before the NECCDC Qualifier event, and must be communicated with Nationals and NECCDL (contact@neccdl.org) at least 24 hours in advance of the competition.
- During the NECCDC Regional, any eight (8) members from the submitted roster can compete on day 1 and a different set of eight (8) on day 2.
  - This change between day 1 and 2 of the NECCDC Regional does not need to be communicated.
  - There are to be no substitutions or additions **during** the competition day without explicit permission from the competition director.


#### 4. Competition Conduct
**n.** Containerizing scored applications is allowed by NECCDC. Keep in mind before migrating, the Orange team will check existing business logic inside the application.

#### 5. Internet Usage
**f.** Competitor Code Repositories 
1. Teams must submit their repositories one week before each competition through a form shared by the League.
2. Once an ongoing competition ends, teams can update <!-- defrost --> their repository. (i.e. updatable between Qualifiers and Regionals)
3. Using GIT is preferred for submitting resources. Repositories should be moved to read-only or archived during the freeze period.

### Beta Test
1. Teams are allowed to download artifacts they generate (scan, inventory, etc.) from systems.
2. Existing files (ex. config file) or images cannot be downloaded/extracted.
3. Screenshots are allowed, and note taking is encouraged.
4. System redeployments are not permitted.
